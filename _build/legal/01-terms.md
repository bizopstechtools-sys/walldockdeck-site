## 5. The Shore Score and the Rule Data

**The Shore Score is a screening, not an inspection.** It is produced from public data and the answers you give us. Nobody visits the property to produce it. It cannot tell you whether your seawall is safe, how long it will last, or what a repair will cost. Only a licensed contractor or engineer who inspects the structure can tell you that. Do not make a repair, purchase, sale or insurance decision on a Shore Score alone.

**The Rule Data is a compiled reference.** We publish each requirement with the code section it came from and the date we checked it. We take that seriously and we do not publish a figure we have not verified. But:

- Codes change, and a rule verified on the date shown may have changed since.
- Cities interpret and apply their own codes, and an official at the counter may read a provision differently.
- A figure stated in one vertical datum is not the same number in another. Always confirm which datum applies.
- Our summary of a rule is not the rule. The ordinance is the rule.

**Confirm with your building department before you rely on anything here**, and before you sign a contract or submit a permit application. If you find something wrong, tell us at hello@walldockdeck.com and we will check it and correct it.
