**Not yet in service.** We do not currently operate a text messaging number. These terms are published so you can read them before the service starts, and so they are in place the moment it does. Until then, every form on this site reaches us by email only.

## The program

**Wall Dock Deck Customer Care.** We send messages about a request you made: confirming it, arranging an inspection with a licensed contractor, answering questions about your property, and notifying you of conditions affecting your shoreline.

We do not send marketing blasts, and we do not message people who did not ask us to.

## How you opt in

One of two ways, and only these two:

1. **You enter your mobile number on a form on this site and tick the consent box.** The box is never pre-ticked. You have to tick it yourself.
2. **You text our published number first.**

We do not buy lists. We do not scrape numbers. We do not take numbers from property records, permit filings or violation notices and text them. We do not use a number you gave us for something else.

## The exact consent wording

This is what appears next to the checkbox, word for word. It is also what we filed with the mobile carriers, and we do not change one without changing the other:

> I agree that WallDockDeck and its contractor partner for my area may contact me by phone, text and email about my property at the number above, including by automated means. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.

## How to stop

**Reply STOP** to any message. We stop immediately and permanently, and we send one confirmation that we have stopped. That is the last message you get.

You can also email hello@walldockdeck.com and ask.

**Reply HELP** for help, or email hello@walldockdeck.com.

**To start again**, reply START, or submit a form and tick the box again.

We will not move you to a different list, message you from a different number, or re-add you from a later form fill in order to get around a STOP.

## What STOP does not do

**STOP stops our messages. It does not reach a contractor who already has your details.**

If you asked us to connect you with a contractor, we gave them your name, phone number and property address. They are an independent business. Our suppression list does not reach into their system.

To stop a contractor contacting you, tell that contractor directly. Tell us too, at hello@walldockdeck.com — we will stop referring you, and we want to know if a contractor is not honouring a request to stop, because it affects whether they stay in our network.

## Privacy

**We never share your mobile number with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.** Your number goes only to the carrier needed to deliver a message you consented to, and — if you asked to be connected — to the contractor for your area.

We do not sell your opt-in data, and we do not share your consent record with anyone for marketing.
