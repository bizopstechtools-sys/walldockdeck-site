## What we set

Very little, because this is a static site with no login.

- **A session identifier**, so that if you move between pages and then submit a form, we know it was one visit rather than three
- **A referral tag**, if you arrived through a real estate agent's link, so the agent gets credit
- **A preference**, remembering a choice you made on the page

These are first-party, they contain no name or contact details, and they are there to make the site work.

## What we do not set

As of the date on this page:

- **No advertising cookies**
- **No third-party analytics**
- **No retargeting or conversion pixels** — no Google Ads tag, no Meta Pixel
- **No session recording or heatmaps**
- **No cookies placed by anyone else**

Because everything we set is essential to the site working, there is **no consent banner**. Asking you to click through a banner for cookies that need no consent would be theatre.

## Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. This site will still work — you may just have to re-enter something you already typed.

We honour the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)**. See [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/your-privacy-choices/).

## Email

Our emails may use a tracking pixel or tagged links to tell us whether a message was opened and whether you clicked. That is not a cookie, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.

## When this changes

**We expect this page to change.** A business that wants to be found will eventually run analytics and probably advertising, and both mean non-essential trackers.

When that happens, we will:

1. **Update this page first**, before the tag goes live
2. **Put a real consent control in place** — an actual choice, with reject as easy as accept, not a banner that only offers "OK"
3. **Set nothing non-essential until you have chosen**
4. **Honour GPC** as an opt-out signal

We are writing this down now, while it would be easy to leave vague, so that you can hold us to it later.
