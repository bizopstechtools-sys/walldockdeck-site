About

Who we are, and how we are paid

WallDockDeck publishes the waterfront building rules for South Florida and connects owners with licensed marine contractors. We do not perform the work ourselves, and we are paid by the contractors we refer to — not by you. Both of those facts change how you should read everything else here, so they go at the top rather than in the small print.

What we do

We publish the rulesThe seawall, dock and waterfront deck requirements for the cities we cover, each with its code section and the date we checked it. Most of this exists only as legal text in a municipal PDF. We publish the number.
We score your shorelineThe Shore Score is a screening based on public data and the answers you give us. It tells you what your answers point to and what to ask next. It is not an engineering inspection and we never call it one.
We connect you to a licensed contractorIf you ask to be connected, we pass your details to a licensed marine contractor covering your area. They are an independent business, not our employee or our agent, and they may contact you directly.
We do not do the workWe refer. We do not build, repair, inspect or engineer anything, and we never quote a price for work.

How we are paid, plainly

Worth knowing before you decide how much weight to give our advice.

Contractors pay us, you do notOur contractor partners pay us for referrals. Everything on this site is free to you, including the guides and the Shore Score.
What that means for our incentivesWe have an interest in you contacting a contractor. We have no interest in which option you choose or how much you spend — we are not paid on the size of the job, which is why we publish what drives a price rather than a price.
Why we publish rules rather than quotesA number without a survey is a guess, and a quote from someone who has not seen your wall is worth nothing. The rules are checkable. That is what we can honestly give you.

Who writes this

JB MarineResearches and verifies the rules published here. Every figure is read out of the primary code, recorded with its section and the month it was checked, and nothing publishes without both. The method is set out in full on our verification page.
Where we coverMonroe, Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties — the Florida Keys to Palm Beach. We publish a city only once we have verified its rules, which is why the list grows rather than starting complete.
What we will not doPublish a figure we cannot source, republish another party’s bid, or present an estimate as a quote.

Are your quotes comparable?

Two seawall quotes rarely cover the same work. Tell us where to reach you and we will send you somewhere to upload them, then tell you what each one leaves out.

We do not sell the work. We have no reason to steer you to the expensive quote.
