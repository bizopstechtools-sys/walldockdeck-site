Bal Harbour · Miami-Dade County

What are the deck setback and railing rules in Bal Harbour?

20 ftpools from the outer face of the seawall (Indian Creek / Biscayne Bay lots)

On Bal Harbour lots fronting Indian Creek or Biscayne Bay, pools sit at least 20 ft from the outer face of the seawall, or farther if the tiebacks reach farther. Any pool work within 40 ft of a bulkhead needs engineer-sealed plans, pilings independent of the bulkhead, and a signed release and indemnification.

Source: Village of Bal Harbour Code Secs. 21-353, 21-127 (Supp. 93); Florida Building Code, Residential, 8th Ed. (2023) R312.1.1-R312.1.3 (ICC Digital Codes) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Waterfront poolsIn the R-1, RM-4, RM-5 and PC districts on lots fronting Indian Creek or Biscayne Bay, pools must be set back at least 20 ft from the outer face of the seawall, or the distance from the seawall face to the inner edge of the tieback, whichever is greater. Code Sec. 21-353(b)(1).
Bulkhead setback areasPool construction within 40 ft of a bulkhead needs plans signed and sealed by a certified professional engineer; pools there must sit on pilings independent of the bulkhead pilings and not rely on soil for lateral support; a special inspector may be required; the owner signs a Release and Indemnification Agreement. Sec. 21-353(b)(2).
R-2 single-family15 ft rear setback; pools at least 10 ft from rear and side setback lines; pool decks are not counted for setbacks; pools and decks screened by a 5 ft wall, fence or hedge. Sec. 21-127.
Special inspectorThe building official may require a registered architect or engineer as special inspector, who must attest by affidavit that the bulkhead's structural integrity was not harmed. Sec. 21-353(b)(2)b.
Guards (state rule)Florida Building Code, Residential, 8th Edition (2023), Sec. R312.1.1-R312.1.3: a guard is required on open sides of a walking surface located more than 30 inches above the floor or grade below at any point within 24 inches of the open edge; it must be at least 36 inches high; openings must not let a 4-inch sphere pass. No local guard amendment was found in this city's code.
