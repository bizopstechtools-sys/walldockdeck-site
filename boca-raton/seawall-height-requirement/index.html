Boca Raton · Palm Beach County

How high does a seawall have to be in Boca Raton?

4.0 – 6.0ft NAVD88

Boca Raton sets a band, not a single number. Any new or replaced seawall or dock must reach 4.0 ft NAVD88 and must not exceed 6.0 ft NAVD88. The requirement lives in the city's Engineering Design Standards Manual rather than the ordinance, which is why it is hard to find.

Source: City of Boca Raton Engineering Design Standards Manual § 7.7, Minimum Criteria for Waterway Features (updated January 2025); Code of Ordinances Ch. 22, Marine Structures · Verified 2026-09

The detail

Minimum4.0 ft NAVD88, for any new or replaced seawall or dock, publicly or privately owned.
Maximum6.0 ft NAVD88.
Build for moreStandard 3 requires the wall to be designed and constructed so a further 12 to 18 inches can be added later and the wall stays structurally sound without altering it.
Watch the datumThe city states its requirement in NAVD88. Contractor drawings in this area are sometimes written to MLW instead, and the two are not interchangeable. Ask which datum a quoted cap elevation refers to before you sign.
Where it is writtenEngineering Design Standards Manual section 7.7, Minimum Criteria for Waterway Features, updated January 2025. Chapter 22 of the Code of Ordinances covers marine structures generally.
InspectionsA zoning location survey must be approved before the cap goes on, and a final engineer report with the cap elevation and photographs is required after construction.
