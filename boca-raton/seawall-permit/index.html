Boca Raton · Palm Beach County

Do I need a permit to repair a seawall in Boca Raton?

Yeswith staged inspections

Boca inspects in a set order and one of them happens before the cap goes on. Miss it and the cap comes off, so the sequence matters more here than the paperwork does.

Source: City of Boca Raton, Seawalls and Marine Structures permitting guidance (Development Services, Zoning); Engineering Design Standards Manual § 7.7; Code of Ordinances Ch. 22 · Verified 2026-09

The detail

Inspection one, before the capA zoning location survey showing the seawall wetface or bulkhead line, the property line and the pile locations. It must be approved before the structural cap and piling inspections can even be scheduled.
Inspection two, after constructionA final engineer report giving the seawall cap elevation, confirming the work matches the permit drawings, with photographs of the finished wall.
If there is a dock or lift tooA separate final marine structure survey, plus the location survey before framing. All three inspections apply when the job covers both.
What the plans must showThe existing wetface or bulkhead line, the property line, the distance from the cap edge and from any marine structure to both, existing and proposed panels and piles, and the finished elevation of the top of the cap.
Where the rules liveChapter 22 of the Code of Ordinances for marine structures, and Engineering Design Standards Manual section 7.7 for the elevations.
Budget for a bondA 2026 contractor proposal for a Coral Gables property carried a $15,000 refundable city bond. Ask Boca early whether yours needs one, because it sits on top of the contract price.
