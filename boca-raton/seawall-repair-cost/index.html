Boca Raton · Palm Beach County

What does a seawall cost in Boca Raton?

$370k – $720kestimated range, same wall

One 280-foot seawall in Boca Raton, priced three ways in 2026. Repairing the panels and restoring the cap came to roughly $370,000. Repairing the panels and replacing the cap outright, roughly $470,000. Building a new wall in front of the old one, roughly $720,000. Same wall, same year. These are estimates, not quotes, and they are rounded. What a seawall costs depends on the condition of what is standing now, water depth, whether a barge can reach the site, the soil, the length of wall, what your city requires and what the permitting agencies ask for once they look. Nobody can price this accurately without a site inspection, a current survey and an engineer's assessment. Use these to get a sense of scale for budgeting, not as a number to hold anyone to.

Source: Rounded from marine construction pricing in this market, 2026. Estimates only. · Verified 2026-09
