What does a seawall cost in Boca Raton?

Three approaches, three pricessame wall, very different jobs

The same seawall can be addressed three structurally different ways, and the spread between them is wider than most owners expect. We do not publish figures, because the right approach depends on a survey. But knowing the three options is what lets you tell whether two bids are even for the same job.

Source: City of Boca Raton Engineering Design Standards Manual Sec. 7.7, Minimum Criteria for Waterway Features (updated January 2025); Code of Ordinances Ch. 22, Marine Structures. Cost drivers only - WallDockDeck does not publish price estimates. · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Option oneRepair the panels and restore the existing cap. The lightest intervention, and only possible if the panels are sound.
Option twoRepair the panels and replace the cap outright. Chosen when the cap has spalled or the elevation has to change.
Option threeBuild a new wall in front of the existing one. The heaviest option, and in Boca Raton it needs an exemption from the city - a permitting risk, not just a line item.
Why bids divergeTwo contractors can quote the same shoreline against different options. Ask which of the three each bid assumes, in writing, before you compare them.
Boca's own constraintThe city's Engineering Design Standards Manual sets a minimum of 4.0 ft NAVD88 and a maximum of 6.0 ft NAVD88, and that applies to any new or replaced seawall or dock, publicly or privately owned. An option that changes elevation has to land inside that band.
What else rides alongAccess ramps, staging and restoration are frequently in one bid and absent from another.
