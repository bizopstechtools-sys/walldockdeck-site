Book an inspection

Have someone look at it

A licensed marine contractor covering your area comes to the property, looks at the wall, the cap, the dock and the ground behind it, and tells you what they see. No charge for the visit, and no obligation after it.

What happens

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them.

Step one

You tell us the address

We check which contractor covers it and what your city requires there.

Step two

They call you

Usually within one business day, to agree a time. That is a target, not a promise.

Step three

They walk the wall

At the property, at low tide where they can, because low tide is when a wall tells the truth.

Step four

You decide

You get what they found. Whether you do anything about it is up to you.

Worth being straight about

Who comes

Not us

We publish the rules and make the introduction. The inspection is done by an independent licensed contractor. They are not our employee or our agent, and we do not supervise their work. The terms say exactly that.

Who pays

Not you

The visit is free to you. Contractors pay us for the introduction. You should know that when you weigh what we tell you.

Your details

Go to one contractor

The one covering your area — not sold to a panel to bid on. You can opt out at any time.

If it is urgent

Do not wait for us

A wall that has moved, a sinkhole, or water where it should not be is not a form. Call a licensed marine contractor, and call your building department if anything looks unsafe.

None of this is required. It just makes the visit worth more.
