Broward County · Seawalls

What are the seawall requirements in Broward County?

5.0ft NAVD88 minimum (4.0 if applied for before 2035)

Broward is the one county in South Florida with a single standard behind every city, and the number most people quote is the wrong one. The minimum is 5 ft NAVD88. The 4 ft figure you will hear is an allowance: an application filed before 1 January 2035 may be permitted at 4 ft, if the barrier is built to reach 5 ft by 1 January 2050. It is not a floor you are entitled to, and cities answer it differently, so the number that applies to you is your city's.

Source: Broward County Code Ch. 39, Art. XXV, Secs. 39-404 to 39-408 (Ord. No. 2020-11, 3-31-20; Municode Supp. 72); Broward County "Build It High, Keep It Dry" regional standard guidance (adoption deadline); City of Hollywood Code Sec. 150.30 (Ord. O-2022-01); Fort Lauderdale ULDR Sec. 47-19.13; Pompano Beach Code § 151.05; Deerfield Beach LDC Sec. 98-87(d)(4)b; Hillsboro Beach Code Sec. 12-268 · Verified 2026-10

The detail

The standardTidal flood barriers must have a minimum elevation of 5 ft NAVD88. Broward County Code Sec. 39-407(a).
The 2035 allowanceApplications for new or substantially repaired barriers submitted before 1 January 2035 may be permitted at 4 ft NAVD88, if designed and constructed to accommodate 5 ft NAVD88 by 1 January 2050. Sec. 39-407(a).
It is not a rightThe code says such applications may be permitted, and Article XXV defines may as permissive. Budget for 5 ft and treat 4 ft as the exception you have to ask for. Secs. 39-406, 39-407(a).
Where it comes fromBroward County Code Chapter 39, Article XXV, Resiliency Standards for Tidal Flood Protection, Secs. 39-404 to 39-408, adopted by Ord. No. 2020-11 on 31 March 2020. Sec. 39-407 sets the elevations; Sec. 39-404 is the purpose statement. Broward County Land Use Plan Policy 2.21.7 sits behind it.
Every city had to adopt itThe county's own guidance says local governments were required to adopt an implementing ordinance by 13 February 2022.
Fort Lauderdale5.0 ft NAVD88 minimum with the same pre-2035 4 ft route, and a cap: the base flood elevation or 6.0 ft NAVD88, whichever is lower. ULDR 47-19.13.D(1), D(12).
Hollywood5 ft NAVD88 minimum with the same pre-2035 4 ft route, plus at least one cubic yard per linear foot of riprap or approved habitat enhancement, and a review of the standard due by 2034. Code Sec. 150.30.
Pompano Beach5 ft NAVD88 minimum and 5 ft 10 in maximum, with no pre-2035 4 ft route in its text. Code § 151.05(A).
Not the only capSeveral Broward cities set a maximum as well as a minimum: Pompano Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Deerfield Beach (7 ft NAVD88 or the required finished grade, whichever is higher, LDC 98-87(d)(4)b) and Hillsboro Beach (the property's base flood elevation, Code Sec. 12-268(D)).
Selling a waterfront homeSale contracts for property in tidally influenced areas executed after 31 December 2020 must carry the county disclosure in capitalised bold type of at least 14 point. Sec. 39-408.
The practical pointBuild to 4 ft today and the wall has to come up again before 2050. Ask what it costs to build to 5 ft now, while the barge is already there.
