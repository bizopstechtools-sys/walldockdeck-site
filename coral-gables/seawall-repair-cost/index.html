Coral Gables · Miami-Dade County

What does a seawall cost in Coral Gables?

~$125kestimated, boat lift

About $125,000 in 2026 for a single boat lift sized to a specific vessel. Coral Gables also required a refundable bond of $15,000 on top of the contract, which catches people out. These are estimates, not quotes, and they are rounded. What a seawall costs depends on the condition of what is standing now, water depth, whether a barge can reach the site, the soil, the length of wall, what your city requires and what the permitting agencies ask for once they look. Nobody can price this accurately without a site inspection, a current survey and an engineer's assessment. Use these to get a sense of scale for budgeting, not as a number to hold anyone to.

Source: Rounded from marine construction pricing in this market, 2026. Estimates only. · Verified 2026-09

The detail

What was in scopeone lift, with capacity, water depth, bunk spacing and decking colour all specified to the boat.
On top of the contracta $15,000 refundable city bond.
Also on the ownerpermit fees paid directly to the agencies, and a benthic survey at around $2,000 if the agencies ask for one.
What moves this number mostthe weight and beam of the boat, and the water depth at the dock.
