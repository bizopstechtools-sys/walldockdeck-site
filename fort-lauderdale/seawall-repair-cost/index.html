What does a seawall cost in Fort Lauderdale?

~$140kestimated, seawall and dock together

About $140,000 in 2026 for roughly 80 linear feet of new seawall and cap together with a new composite dock of about 660 square feet, including demolition of the old dock and piles. These are estimates, not quotes, and they are rounded. What a seawall costs depends on the condition of what is standing now, water depth, whether a barge can reach the site, the soil, the length of wall, what your city requires and what the permitting agencies ask for once they look. Nobody can price this accurately without a site inspection, a current survey and an engineer's assessment. Use these to get a sense of scale for budgeting, not as a number to hold anyone to.

Source: Rounded from marine construction pricing in this market, 2026. Estimates only. · Verified 2026-09
