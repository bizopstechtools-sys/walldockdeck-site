What does a seawall cost in Fort Lauderdale?

Scope first, then priceno credible number before a survey

A seawall and a dock priced together are a different job from either one alone, and the number moves on things you can establish before anyone quotes. We do not publish a figure, because a price given without a survey of what is standing is a guess wearing a dollar sign. What we can tell you is exactly what moves it.

Source: Cost drivers only. WallDockDeck does not publish price estimates: a credible number requires a survey of the existing structure. Confirm all figures with a licensed marine contractor. · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Condition of what is standingWhether the existing wall can be repaired or has to be replaced is the single biggest fork in the price. A survey answers it; a phone call cannot.
Demolition and removalTaking out an old dock and its timber piles is its own line. Piles pulled, not cut, cost more and are usually the right answer.
AccessWhether a barge can reach the shoreline, or the work has to come through the property, changes mobilisation before a single panel is set.
Elevation targetBuilding to 4 ft NAVD88 now versus straight to 5 ft changes steel and concrete today, and decides whether the wall comes up again before 2050.
Decking selectionComposite varies widely by product. Ask for the per-square-foot rate and the exact product name in the bid.
Return wallsOften not known until the wall is exposed. Ask for it priced as a conditional line rather than discovered later.
What to ask forA bid broken into demolition, wall, cap, dock and decking as separate lines. Two bids far apart almost always differ in one of them.
