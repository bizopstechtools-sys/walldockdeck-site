What are the storm and debris rules in Fort Lauderdale?

Boat owner's jobno citywide boat-removal order

Fort Lauderdale's code puts storm protection of a boat on its owner: a vessel at a municipal dock space must be protected by its owner once a hurricane warning is issued for Broward County. Boarding up openings for hurricane preparation during a watch or warning is carved out of the boarding-certificate rules, and a tidal flood barrier left in disrepair after a storm is a citable offence with fixed repair deadlines.

Source: City of Fort Lauderdale Code of Ordinances Secs. 8-120, 18-9; ULDR Sec. 47-19.13 (Municode) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

City dock spaceIt is the responsibility of the owner of a vessel moored at any municipal dock space to protect the vessel upon the issuance of a hurricane warning for Broward County - Code Sec. 8-120 (Ord. C-91-69)
Boarding upWeather and hurricane preparation when a hurricane or tropical storm watch or warning has been issued by the National Weather Service is listed as an exception to the boarding-certificate requirement - Code Sec. 18-9(b)
Damaged seawallAll property owners shall maintain a tidal flood barrier in good repair; one that lets tidal water flow unimpeded through it onto a neighbour or the right-of-way is presumed in disrepair. It is a citable offence: show progress within sixty (60) days of the citation and complete repairs within three hundred sixty-five (365) days - ULDR Sec. 47-19.13.D.2
