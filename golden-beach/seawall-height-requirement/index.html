Golden Beach · Miami-Dade County

How high does a seawall have to be in Golden Beach?

4.425ft NAVD88 (single required value: "constructed at an elevation of"; not labelled minimum or maximum)

All new and replacement seawalls must be built to 4.425 ft NAVD88. Existing legal nonconforming seawalls must be brought up when the home is substantially improved or demolished, the wall is materially altered, or the property is sold.

Source: Golden Beach Code of Ordinances Sec. 46-85(a) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Required heightAll new and replacement seawalls shall be constructed at an elevation of 4.425 ft NAVD-88 - Golden Beach Code Sec. 46-85(a)
DocksMin 3.425 ft, max 4.425 ft NAVD-88; finish material max 3 in. thick - Sec. 46-85(a)
Nonconforming wallsMust comply on >50% improvement, demolition of primary structure, or material alteration - Sec. 46-86(b)
SalePre-sale engineer report must state cap height in NAVD-88; deficiencies, including raising the cap if not per NAVD-88 requirements, must be corrected and a building permit application submitted within 180 days after closing - Sec. 46-86(b)(5)
SurveysPermit and as-built surveys show cap elevations in NAVD-88 at 20-ft intervals - Sec. 46-79
