How high does a seawall have to be in Gulf Stream?

No minimumset in local code

Gulf Stream sets no minimum seawall height. It caps new or replacement seawalls on Intracoastal-connected water at 8 ft NGVD (or the NAVD equivalent).

Source: Town of Gulf Stream Code, Ch. 70 Art. IV Sec. 70-78 Seawall height; Ch. 52 Art. II Seawalls, Bulkheads, Etc. (Ord. 22-9, 10-28-22); Sec. 52-28; Ch. 66 Sec. 66-369 · Verified 2026-10

The detail

MinimumNone set
MaximumNo greater than 8 ft NGVD or its equivalent in NAVD - Sec. 70-78
Seawall requiredOwners on tidal/brackish water must bulkhead their frontage (not ocean) - Sec. 52-26
PlansTown commission approval - Sec. 52-27
HistoryThe cap was first set by Ord. No. 15/9 (12 Feb 2015) and amended by Ord. No. 22-9 (28 Oct 2022) - Sec. 70-78
Turbidity screensDuring clearing, excavating, jetting and backfilling the contractor must fully enclose the site with screens reaching the bottom, left in place 24 hours after work stops or until turbidity returns to normal - Sec. 52-28
Docks and liftsBoats dock parallel to the seawall only. Docks may be no higher than the abutting seawall or 5.5 ft above mean sea level, whichever is greater; lift equipment max 5 ft above the seawall, and no part of a lifted vessel more than 10 ft above it - Sec. 66-369(4), (6)
