Hallandale Beach · Broward County

How high does a seawall have to be in Hallandale Beach?

5.0ft NAVD88

New, substantially repaired or substantially rehabilitated seawalls in Hallandale Beach must reach at least 5.0 ft NAVD88. Applications filed before 1 Jan 2035 can be approved at 4.0 ft NAVD88 if the wall is designed to be raised to 5.0 ft by 2050. The city code sets no maximum seawall height.

Source: Hallandale Beach Code of Ordinances Ch. 8, Art. III, Div. 3, Sec. 8-90 (Ord. No. 2020-030); Secs. 8-91 and 8-111 · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Minimum5.0 ft NAVD88 - Code Sec. 8-90(a)
InterimApplications submitted before 1 Jan 2035 may be permitted at 4.0 ft NAVD88 if designed and constructed to accommodate 5.0 ft NAVD88 by 1 Jan 2050 - Sec. 8-90(a)
Applies tonew tidal flood barriers and substantial repair/rehab; not oceanfront seaward of the CCCL - Sec. 8-89
Side containmentowners must add side containment barriers as needed to keep rainwater on their property - Sec. 8-90(c)
Whole shorelinesubstantial repair/rehab must cover the entire shoreline - Sec. 8-90(f)
AdoptedOrd. No. 2020-030, 18 Nov 2020 - Sec. 8-89 to 8-91
SellingOwners and buyers must follow the Broward County Code Sec. 39-408 tidal-area disclosure in sale contracts - Sec. 8-91
Condo conversionsBefore rental units are sold as condominiums, the owner's certificate-of-compliance application must list any known seawall defects or deficiencies, and a licensed building contractor must certify the repairs - Sec. 8-111(c)(5), (d)
The city's callNo seawall is required where, in the city's opinion, other flood protection does the job just as well - Sec. 8-90(i)
