Hillsboro Beach · Broward County

What are the deck setback and railing rules in Hillsboro Beach?

15 ftfrom the Intracoastal right-of-way line or seawall for pools and buildings; flat patios may reach it

Hillsboro Beach keeps buildings and pools 15 ft back from the easterly right-of-way line of the Intracoastal Waterway (or from a seawall built east of it). Flat, open patios with no footings are the exception: they may extend all the way to that Intracoastal line.

Source: Town of Hillsboro Beach Code Secs. 12-264, 12-124 (Supp. 16; Ord. 2018-05); Florida Building Code, Residential, 8th Ed. (2023) R312.1.1-R312.1.3 (ICC Digital Codes) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

PatiosFlat and open patios with no footings may extend 15 ft east of the easterly building line and to the easterly right-of-way line of the Intracoastal Waterway. Code Sec. 12-264(D).
PoolsNo closer than 15 ft to any side property line or 45 ft to the centerline of State Highway A1A; not east of the easterly building line or closer than 15 ft to the easterly ICW right-of-way line, or 15 ft east of any seawall erected east of that line; measured from the water's edge of the pool. Sec. 12-264(A).
BuildingsNo building closer than 15 ft to the easterly right-of-way line of the Intracoastal Waterway, or 15 ft east of any seawall east of it. Sec. 12-124(D).
Fuel and inspectionAbove-ground fuel tanks for pool heaters and generators follow the town's setbacks; pools cannot be filled before inspection unless the building department requires it for testing. Sec. 12-264(B)-(C).
UpkeepPools, jacuzzis, spas, hot tubs and fountains must keep pumps and filters working and the water clear enough to see the bottom at the deepest point. Sec. 12-264(E)-(F).
Guards (state rule)Florida Building Code, Residential, 8th Edition (2023), Sec. R312.1.1-R312.1.3: a guard is required on open sides of a walking surface located more than 30 inches above the floor or grade below at any point within 24 inches of the open edge; it must be at least 36 inches high; openings must not let a 4-inch sphere pass. No local guard amendment was found in this city's code.
