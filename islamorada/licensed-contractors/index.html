How do I check a marine contractor in Islamorada?

Register with the VillageCityView business account; annual fees

Islamorada requires state-certified and registered contractors with a valid Monroe County certificate of competency to register with the Village before working there, using the Contractor Registration Checklist and a CityView business portal account. Contractors must upload renewals and pay annual fees to stay active, and permit applications are accepted only electronically in CityView.

Source: Islamorada, Village of Islands Contractor Registration page; Building Services page; Monroe County Contractors Examining Board page; New Certificate of Competency checklist (rev. 07/23/2026); File A Contractor Complaint page; F.S. 489.105, 489.126, 489.141, 489.1425, 489.143, 440.02, 440.05, 501.021, 501.025, 205.065 (2026); Fla. Admin. Code R. 61G4-15.100; DBPR Construction Industry page and Verify a Licensee; DFS Division of Workers' Compensation construction exemption page and data portal · Verified 2026-10

The detail

RegistrationCreate a CityView Portal account for the business with a business email, follow 'Contractor License Registration (Required to work in the Village)', then activate the Business Portal user account - Contractor Registration page
VerifyState licences through DBPR (1-850-487-1395); county licences through the Monroe County Licensing Coordinator, 305-289-2583
Past permitsCityView Portal (cvportal.us/CityViewPortal); Building Department, 2nd floor, 86800 Overseas Highway, which keeps permit records and contractor registrations - Building Services page
County certificatesMonroe's Contractors Examining Board (County Code Secs. 6-263 to 6-270) issues certificates of competency, including Seawall & Small Dock (Marine Const.) Engineering Class II (c), which needs three years' experience. County certificate holders may work only where the county has a reciprocal agreement
County look-upSearch county licences in MCeSearch (mcesearch.monroecounty-fl.gov) or call the Monroe County Licensing Coordinator, 305-289-2583
State licenceSearch the company and the qualifier on DBPR's Verify a Licensee (myfloridalicense.com). A licence number starting with C is a state certification valid anywhere in Florida; one starting with R is a registration, good only where the holder met local competency rules - F.S. 489.105(8),(10) and the DBPR licensing page
Who may build a seawall or dockA certified general contractor's scope is unlimited (489.105(3)(a)). The state also certifies five marine specialties: marine specialty, marine seawall work, marine bulkhead work, marine dock work and marine pile driving - Rule 61G4-15.100(1)(b)-(f)
Workers' compIn construction a single employee makes coverage mandatory (440.02(20)(b)2). Up to three officers or LLC members owning at least 10% may file exemptions (440.02(18)(b)2), and the exemption certificate itself says it is not a DBPR licence (440.05(4)). Check it on the DWC data portal's Coverage and Exemptions search (dwcdataportal.fldfs.com)
Recovery fundFor contracts signed on or after 1 July 2024 the Homeowners' Construction Recovery Fund pays at most $100,000 per claim against a Division I contractor (general, building, residential) and $30,000 against a Division II contractor, which includes the marine specialties; the lifetime caps are $2 million and $600,000 per licensee - 489.143(3),(6). Nothing is paid if the contractor was unlicensed when you signed - 489.141(2)(d) - and residential contracts over $2,500 must print the fund notice - 489.1425
DepositsA contractor who takes more than 10% up front on residential work must apply for permits within 30 days and start within 90 days of the permits issuing, unless you agreed to longer in writing - 489.126(2)(a)
Three-day cancelIf the deal was a home solicitation sale over $25 - signed away from the seller's place of business and not the result of your own request for specific goods or services - you can cancel in writing until midnight of the third business day; Sundays and federal holidays don't count - 501.021, 501.025
Business tax is not a licenceA contractor pays business tax where its permanent office is, and no other city or county may levy a business tax or an equivalent registration fee for temporary work there - 205.065
