Juno Beach · Palm Beach County

What are the storm and debris rules in Juno Beach?

Remove on a watchbeach-stored watercraft

Juno Beach allows only non-motorized watercraft to be stored on the beach, by town permit, and owners must remove them on notice of a hurricane watch; failing to comply within 48 hours can cost the permit and lead to impoundment. After a declared disaster the town clears debris from public and private streets, with private-road owners holding the town harmless.

Source: Town of Juno Beach Code of Ordinances Secs. 18-100, 22-1 (Municode) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Beach storageStorage of nonmotorized watercraft on the beach requires a town permit; no motorized watercraft may be stored on the beach or dune - Code Sec. 18-100(a)
Hurricane watchUpon notification of a hurricane watch from the county's division of emergency management or area news weather stations, the owner shall remove the watercraft - Sec. 18-100(d)(4); failure to comply within 48 hours results in permit revocation and may result in boat impoundment - Sec. 18-100(d)(6)
Street debrisAfter the mayor declares a state of emergency, the town may clear debris from public and private streets and rights-of-way, including private communities; owners of private roads hold the town harmless, and removal is limited to eliminating an imminent threat - Sec. 22-1 (Ord. 598)
