Jupiter · Palm Beach County

How high does a seawall have to be in Jupiter?

No minimumset in local code

Jupiter sets no minimum seawall height. It caps new bulkheads at the property's FEMA base flood elevation, and a bulkhead more than 3 ft above mean high water must be fully faced with riprap or mangroves.

Source: Town of Jupiter Code of Ordinances, Ch. 27 Zoning, Art. XI Div. 25 Marine Facilities, Sec. 27-2624 (Supp. 24, online 10/2/2026) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

MinimumNone set in Sec. 27-2624 (Shoreline stabilization)
MaximumTop of new bulkhead must be at or below the property's required base flood elevation on current FEMA maps - Sec. 27-2624(8)
Exemptions to maxProperties east of the Coastal Construction Control Line; Riverwalk properties where ADA slope cannot be met - Sec. 27-2624(8)a-b
Riprap triggerTop more than 3 ft above mean high water line requires 100% riprap/mangrove facing - Sec. 27-2624(1)c, (2)d
LocationReplacement within 18 in. waterward of existing permitted bulkhead (one-time waterward move since 6/16/2015) - Sec. 27-2624(1)-(2)
Riprap placementRiprap must cover the bottom half of the bulkhead, sloped no steeper than 2:1, and may not reach more than 8 ft waterward of the mean high water line. No filter cloth is needed - Sec. 27-2624(5)b
Mangrove follow-upWithin two years of final inspection, mangroves must be established on at least 10% of the riprap-faced shoreline; if not, 20% of the shoreline must then be planted - Sec. 27-2624(5)c-d
Old unpermitted wallsWhen replacing an unpermitted bulkhead, a dated survey or historical aerial proving it existed before 20 Dec 2005 excuses the riprap, as long as the new top stays within 3 ft of mean high water. Wood or other degradable walls must be removed - Sec. 27-2624(2)f, (2)h
