What are the storm and debris rules in Key Biscayne?

Before the warninglandscape debris must be gone

Key Biscayne bans putting yard waste on or within ten feet of the right-of-way once a hurricane watch is issued, unless the Village Manager instructs otherwise in writing. Debris can sit on your own swale only for a licensed private hauler, and only if pickup is finished before a hurricane warning is issued, at the owner's cost.

Source: Village of Key Biscayne Code of Ordinances Sec. 8-301, Article V Hurricane Protection Measures (Municode) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Hurricane watchOnce an official hurricane watch is issued by the National Weather Service for an area including the Village, no person shall place yard waste - clippings, trees, palm fronds, leaves, branches - on any portion of the public right-of-way or within ten feet of it, unless instructed in writing by the Village Manager - Code Sec. 8-301(a)
Private hauler exceptionTemporary placement on the swale beside the parcel that produced it is allowed for pickup by a licensed private hauler, so long as pickup and removal is fully accomplished before the National Weather Service issues an official hurricane warning and at the private property owner's sole cost - Sec. 8-301(a)
