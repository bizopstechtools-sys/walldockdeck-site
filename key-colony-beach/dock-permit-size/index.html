Key Colony Beach · Monroe County

How big a dock can I build in Key Colony Beach?

5 ft into the canaldefault; 30-125 ft in named subdivisions

In most of Key Colony Beach a dock may extend only 5 ft into a canal or other waterway, though it may run the full length of the shoreline. Specific subdivisions get their own limits: 40 ft in Marina Subdivision No. 2, 35 ft in the Yacht Club Subdivision, 30 ft for listed lots on Key Colony Channel, and out to 5 ft of water depth in Coury No. Two and Vista Del Sol on Vaca Cut and Shelter Bay. One dock per lot, and no new boat slips at all.

Source: City of Key Colony Beach Code Ch. 5, Secs. 5-35 (through Ord. 429-2012), 5-37 to 5-45 (5-43 through Ord. 2023-484; 5-44 through Ord. 2025-502); F.A.C. 62-302.700(9)(i)13 (rule effective 11-25-2025); Fla. Stat. 403.813(1)(b) (2026); 33 CFR 322.3(a) · Verified 2026-10
