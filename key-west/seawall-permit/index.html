Key West · Monroe County

Do I need a permit to repair a seawall in Key West?

Yescity building permit

Yes. Key West requires a building permit for any work valued over $1,000, and its code says shoreline bulkheads are installed under a permit from the building official. City rules allow hardening only where erosion is a critical peril and vegetation has failed, with riprap first and generally no vertical seawalls; since 1 Jan 2025 incomplete applications are refused.

Source: Key West Code Secs. 110-182, 110-190, 110-192, 118-361 (Supp. 99); Building Permit Requirements page; s. 403.813(1)(e), F.S. (2026); 33 CFR 322.3(a) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

CityA permit is required before any work or construction of any kind, permanent or temporary, valued over $1,000 including materials and labor, at the building official's discretion; incomplete applications are not accepted since 1 Jan 2025; exterior work in the Historic District also needs a Certificate of Appropriateness - City Building Permit Requirements page. Bulkheads, piers and docks along the shoreline are installed under permit from the building official, and bulkheads may not be built below mean high water unless the controlling federal or state agency permits it - Code Sec. 118-361. Hardening only where erosion is a critical peril to upland property and vegetation has failed; riprap first; generally not vertical seawalls; other agencies with jurisdiction must approve first - Sec. 110-182(c). The city requests jurisdictional determinations from the appropriate agencies before issuing building permits - Sec. 110-190(5). Minor maintenance or emergency repair to existing structures is exempt from that article's coastal resource rules - Sec. 110-192(a)(1)
StateRestoring a seawall at its previous location, upland of it, or within 18 inches waterward is exempt from a state environmental resource permit; the exemption does not replace permission to use state-owned submerged land or county and city requirements, and does not cover chapter 161 coastal permits - s. 403.813(1), (1)(e), F.S.
FederalA U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Department of the Army) authorization is required for structures or work in or affecting navigable waters, unless exempt or covered by a nationwide or regional general permit - 33 CFR 322.3(a) (Rivers and Harbors Act s. 10)
