Your Privacy Choices

How to stop us giving your information to contractors, and the other rights you have.

Last updated 2026-10-02 · Version 2026-10-02

1. We disclose your information to contractors, and you can stop us

Most companies put a page here saying they do not sell your information. We are not going to do that, because it would not be true.

When you ask us to connect you with a contractor, we give that contractor your personal information. Contractors pay us for referrals. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and under similar laws in other states, that is likely a "sale" of personal information, and at minimum it is a "sharing" or disclosure that you have the right to opt out of.

So rather than argue about the label, we will just tell you what happens and give you a way to stop it.
What we disclose Your name, email address, phone number, property address, and what you told us about the seawall, dock or deck
Who receives it Independent licensed marine and general contractors covering your area
Why So they can contact you about the work you enquired about
What they do with it They use it for their own purposes, under their own privacy policy. We do not control that
Do we get paid Yes. Contractors pay us for referrals. That is how this site is free to you

We do not disclose your information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not run advertising or tracking cookies. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

We never disclose your mobile number to anyone for their marketing. It goes only to the carrier delivering a message you consented to, and to the contractor for your area if you asked to be connected.

2. How to opt out

Any of these works. You do not need an account and you do not have to explain yourself.

Email us. Send hello@walldockdeck.com with the subject line Do Not Sell or Share. Include the email address or phone number you used so we can find your record.

Tell us on the form. Every form that collects contact details says, at the point of collection, that your details go to a contractor. If you want the guide but not the referral, say so in the notes field and we will honour it.

Use a browser signal. We honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC). If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out for that browser automatically, with nothing further needed from you.

What happens then. We stop disclosing your information to contractors. You keep access to everything else — every guide, sheet and rule page stays available. Opting out never costs you anything and never gets you a worse service.

What we cannot undo. If a contractor already has your details, opting out stops any future disclosure but does not remove you from that contractor's records. We will tell you which contractor received your information and when, so you can contact them directly. We would rather tell you this up front than have you discover it later.

3. Your other rights

Depending on your state, you may have the right to:

  • Know what we collect, why, and who we disclose it to
  • Access a copy in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete what we hold
  • Opt out of sale or sharing, as above
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as those laws define it, so there is nothing to limit — but if that changes, this page changes
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these

These rights exist under the laws of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Minnesota and Maryland, and under the Florida Digital Bill of Rights. Which ones you have depends on where you live and on which laws apply to a business our size — but we will honour a request from anyone, in any state, rather than make you work out whether your state's threshold applies to us.

4. How to make a request

Email hello@walldockdeck.com, subject line Privacy request.

Tell us which right you are exercising, and give us the email address or phone number you used so we can find your record. We verify your identity before we act, because the alternative is handing your file to whoever asks for it.

We respond within 45 days. If we need more time we will tell you inside that 45 days and take no more than a further 45.

An authorised agent may act for you with written proof. We may still confirm directly with you.

5. Appeals

If we decline your request, we will tell you why.

You may appeal by replying to our response. A different person reviews the appeal than the one who made the first decision, and we respond within the time the law allows.

If you are still not satisfied, you may contact the Florida Attorney General at myfloridalegal.com, or the attorney general of your own state.
