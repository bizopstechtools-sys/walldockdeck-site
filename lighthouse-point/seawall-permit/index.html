Do I need a permit to repair a seawall in Lighthouse Point?

Yes, plus county licensecity permit + county license

Yes. Lighthouse Point's Building Department lists repairs to seawalls among projects that need a building permit, and even owner-builders must use a licensed contractor for piling work. Starting without a permit brings a stop work order and double fees. Broward County also requires its own Environmental Resource License, often a general license for a repair in the same place.

Source: City of Lighthouse Point Building & Zoning FAQ; Broward County Code Ch. 27 Art. XI Secs. 27-333, 27-336 (Supp. 72); s. 403.813(1)(e), F.S. (2026); 33 CFR 322.3(a) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

City'Repairs to seawalls' are on the city's list of work that needs a building permit; owner-builders may pull most permits, but all electrical, roofing and piling work must be done by a licensed contractor; contractors must register with the city before applying; work begun without a permit draws a stop work order, a code enforcement violation notice and twice the original fee - City Building & Zoning FAQ. Online applications through the city's SmartGov permitting portal - same page After an unpermitted job is permitted, covered work must be uncovered for inspection before the stop work order is lifted, unless the building official lets an engineer or architect inspect it and accept responsibility in a letter - same page. Single-family permits issued after 1 July 2026 are valid for 1 year, each passed inspection extends the permit, and renewals cost 50% of the permit cost the first time and 100% the second; a permit that lapses a third time must be applied for again - same page
Broward CountyConstruction in regulated aquatic resources needs a county Environmental Resource License (ERL) - Broward County Code Sec. 27-333(a)(1). Repairing an existing functional seawall no more than 1 ft waterward of its authorized location, or a new wall within 1 ft of the old wet face with the old wall left in place, qualifies for a general license (Sec. 27-336(a)(1)b.), as do refacing, crack filling, seepage holes and other minor repairs (n.) and raising the cap in the same footprint (o.); the code says a general license is issued within 10 days of a complete application - Sec. 27-336(a)(1)
StateRestoring a seawall at its previous location, upland of it, or within 18 inches waterward is exempt from a state environmental resource permit; the exemption does not replace permission to use state-owned submerged land or county and city requirements, and does not cover chapter 161 coastal permits - s. 403.813(1), (1)(e), F.S.
FederalA U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Department of the Army) authorization is required for structures or work in or affecting navigable waters, unless exempt or covered by a nationwide or regional general permit - 33 CFR 322.3(a) (Rivers and Harbors Act s. 10)
