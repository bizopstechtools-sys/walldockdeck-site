What are the boat lift rules in Marathon?

Allowed; lift max 5 ft above dockcovered lifts to 20 ft above MHW

Marathon limits the frame of a boat lift beside a dock or seawall to 5 feet above the dock's highest walking surface or the seawall cap, not counting boat guides; pile-mounted davits may reach 8.5 feet. Covered lifts without side walls are allowed up to 20 feet above mean high water, but not over protected seagrass or hardbottom, and a lift is not permitted where it would harm nearby natural resources.

Source: City of Marathon LDR (Code App. A) Sec. 104.64 (municipalcodeonline.com, read 3 Oct 2026); s. 403.813(1)(s), F.S. (2026); 33 CFR 322.3(a) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Height of liftNo part of a boat lift beside a dock or seawall, except boat guides, may exceed 5 feet above the highest walking surface of the dock or seawall cap - LDR Sec. 104.64 (docking facilities)
DavitsPile-mounted davits no higher than 8.5 feet above the highest walking surface of the dock - Sec. 104.64
CoversCovered boat lifts without side walls may be permitted; covered lifts over submerged bottoms with protected living marine resources are prohibited; maximum 20 feet from mean high water to the highest point - Sec. 104.64
Natural resourcesBoat lifts are not permitted where they can reasonably be expected to harm natural resources in the immediate vicinity - Sec. 104.64
Tie-downsDocks with boat lifts, davits or similar mechanisms must have cleats, rings or similar points to secure the boat out of the water in high wind - Sec. 104.64
ReachDocks no longer than 100 feet from mean low water or 10% of the water body width; dock or mooring structure plus moored vessel no more than 25% of the navigable portion - Sec. 104.64
How manyOne dock structure per single-family property under common ownership - Sec. 104.64
PermitsAll federal, state and water management district permits before the city building permit - Sec. 104.64
State and federalFloating boat lifts and floating vessel platforms that meet s. 403.813(1)(s), F.S. (they float at all times, hold the vessel out of the water, and either sit inside a previously permitted slip or total no more than 500 sq ft - 200 sq ft in Outstanding Florida Waters - beside a qualifying dock or bulkhead) need no state permit; a local government may then ask only for a one-time registration unless the platform is attached to a bulkhead with no dock. Structures in navigable waters otherwise need Army Corps authorization unless exempt or under a general permit - 33 CFR 322.3(a).
