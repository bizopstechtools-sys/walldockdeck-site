What are the storm and debris rules in Marathon?

Right of entryto clear debris from private canals

Marathon gives the City a right of entry to remove disaster debris from canals, private or public - and from upland lots next to a canal when needed for equipment - when the City Manager finds an immediate threat, including submerged or partially submerged vessels making a canal impassable. Commercial dock applications must include an approved hurricane preparedness plan.

Source: City of Marathon Code Sec. 26-103.1 and LDR Sec. 104.64 (municipalcodeonline.com) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

CanalsThe City has the right of entry and authority to remove disaster-generated debris on or in canals, whether privately or publicly owned, when the debris poses an immediate threat; the right extends to adjacent upland private property needed for equipment access - Code Sec. 26-103.1 (Ord. 2017-08; 2023-01)
Threat testIncludes contaminants likely to impair city waterways, and debris - including submerged or partially submerged vessels - rendering canals impassible or extremely dangerous to vessels or blocking first responders - Sec. 26-103.1
Commercial docksApplications must include an approved hurricane preparedness plan; all docks, piers, seawalls and revetments are designed by a professional engineer - LDR Sec. 104.64
