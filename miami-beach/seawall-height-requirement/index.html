Miami Beach · Miami-Dade County

How high does a seawall have to be in Miami Beach?

5.7ft NAVD88

Required for a new seawall, or an existing one undergoing substantial improvement. A wall being repaired below that threshold may be built to 4.0 ft NAVD88, but only if it is designed and constructed to accommodate a future 5.7 ft.

Source: City of Miami Beach Code Sec. 54-62(a); Ordinance 2025-4754, adopted 23 July 2025 · Verified 2026-09

The detail

Interim elevation4.0 ft NAVD88, allowed only for repairs that are less than substantial, and only if the wall is built to accommodate a future 5.7 ft.
What counts as substantialRepairs costing $500 or more per linear foot. The threshold was raised from $300 in July 2025.
DatumNAVD88 — the North American Vertical Datum of 1988. A figure quoted in MLW is not the same number.
Current ordinance2025-4754, adopted 23 July 2025, amending Chapter 54, Article III.
