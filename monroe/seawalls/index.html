Monroe County · Seawalls

What are the seawall requirements in Monroe County?

No county height ruleconfirmed, not assumed

Monroe is the only county of the four where we can say with certainty that no elevation standard exists, because we read every mention of seawall and bulkhead in the county's code to establish it. That does not make the Keys permissive. Monroe regulates something the other three counties largely do not - whether you may build a vertical wall at all.

Source: Monroe County Land Development Code Sec. 118-12(k), Shoreline Setbacks; Monroe County Comprehensive Plan Policies 210.1.6 and 212.5.1-212.5.4; Monroe County Code of Ordinances Sec. 6-100(b) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

No minimum, no maximumMonroe County Land Development Code Sec. 118-12(k) sets no elevation and no datum for a seawall or bulkhead. Every seawall and bulkhead reference in the Code of Ordinances, the Land Development Code and the Comprehensive Plan was checked.
The real restriction is locationA vertical seawall or bulkhead is permitted only to stabilise a severely eroding shoreline, and only on manmade canals, channels or basins. Sec. 118-12(k)(3).
It is a last resortPermitted only if native vegetation, or riprap and filter cloth, is not a feasible means to control erosion. Sec. 118-12(k)(3).
Open water is closedNo new seawalls, bulkheads or other hardened vertical structures are permitted on open water. Comprehensive Plan Policy 210.1.6 goes further - seawalls are prohibited on any beach or open-water shoreline.
Repair is protectedA lawfully existing deteriorated seawall may be repaired or replaced, and is exempt from the nonsubstantial-improvements limit, except on known or potential sea turtle nesting beaches. The work must keep the existing footprint to the maximum extent practicable. Sec. 118-12(k)(4).
Turtles outrank the wallNo seawall, bulkhead or riprap on or waterward of a beach berm complex that is known or potential marine turtle nesting area, as determined by the County Biologist. Sec. 118-12(k)(7).
Riprap and mangrovesRiprap at the toe wherever feasible, and barriers placed landward of existing mangroves whenever feasible. Sec. 118-12(k)(5) and (6).
The one height-adjacent ruleExisting grade landward of the bulkhead must be at least six inches lower than the top of the bulkhead. Sec. 118-12(k)(2). That fixes grade against the cap. It does not tell you how high the cap must be.
A wall with no houseA seawall without a principal use may have a cap no more than two feet wide. Sec. 118-12(k)(1).
State and federal come firstAll such projects require state and/or federal permits before construction and before the county issues a Notice to Proceed. Sec. 118-12(k)(9). A separate county permit is required for a seawall. Code of Ordinances Sec. 6-100(b).
One city does set a heightKey Colony Beach requires 5.5 ft above mean sea level. It is the only jurisdiction in the county with a seawall elevation standard.
