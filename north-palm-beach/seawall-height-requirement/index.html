How high does a seawall have to be in North Palm Beach?

4ft NAVD88

New and substantially rebuilt seawalls in North Palm Beach need a cap at least 4 ft NAVD88, or 5 ft NAVD88 on the Intracoastal, Lake Worth or the ocean in a FEMA flood zone. Repairs under 50% of replacement cost are exempt.

Source: Village of North Palm Beach Code, Ch. 5 Boats, Docks and Waterways, Art. III Div. 3, Sec. 5-72 (Ord. 2019-07; Ord. 2026-02, 1-22-26); Secs. 5-70, 5-73, 5-83 · Verified 2026-10

Cap and positionThe cap may extend no more than 3 ft from the wet face or rear property line; a rebuilt wall may sit up to 18 in. waterside of the property line, further only by council fill permit - Sec. 5-72(1)-(2)
