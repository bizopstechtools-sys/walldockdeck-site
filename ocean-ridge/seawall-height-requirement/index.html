Ocean Ridge · Palm Beach County

How high does a seawall have to be in Ocean Ridge?

5ft NAVD88

New, substantially repaired or substantially rehabilitated seawalls in Ocean Ridge must have a top (including cap) of at least 5 ft NAVD88. Atlantic Ocean frontage is excluded.

Source: Town of Ocean Ridge Code, Ch. 64 Zoning, Art. IV, Sec. 64-82 (Ord. 2025-03, 6-2-2025); Sec. 64-81 · Verified 2026-10

Cap widthNo more than 2 ft from the wet face or the property line, whichever is greater - Sec. 64-82(a)(4)
How far waterwardWhere the property line abuts the waterway line, a rebuilt wall may project at most 18 in. waterward, with no further projections - Sec. 64-82(a)(3)a
Cost test50% of fair market replacement cost as certified by a licensed Florida engineer; docks, piles and footers are excluded unless poured monolithically with the wall - Sec. 64-82(c)(5)
Navigation and easementsWhere the lot runs past the waterway line, a new or rebuilt wall may not unreasonably interfere with navigation or existing easement rights - Sec. 64-82(a)(2), (a)(3)b
NeglectA seawall, bulkhead or dock left in disrepair or ruin so it causes flooding or damage is declared a public nuisance; failing to maintain is a citable violation under the town's community standards procedures - Sec. 64-81, 64-82(e)
No walls on empty lotsA new seawall is not allowed where none existed and no principal structure exists, except to protect government property - Sec. 64-82(g)
