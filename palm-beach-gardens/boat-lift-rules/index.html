Palm Beach Gardens · Palm Beach County

What are the boat lift rules in Palm Beach Gardens?

No lift-specific rule; davits within side setbackdocks 50 ft or 10%

Palm Beach Gardens does not regulate boat lifts by name. Its residential dock standards keep docks, mooring pilings, davits, dolphins and other mooring facilities out of the district side setback, cap docks at 50 feet or 10% of the waterway, allow one dock or mooring per residential lot, and bar a building permit until every federal, state and regional approval is in hand.

Source: City of Palm Beach Gardens LDR Sec. 78-159(40) (Supp. 53); s. 403.813(1)(s), F.S. (2026); 33 CFR 322.3(a) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

DavitsDocks, mooring pilings, davits, dolphins or other mooring facilities may not extend beyond the side setback of the zoning district - LDR Sec. 78-159(40)g.5
ReachDocks no more than 50 feet from the high-water shoreline or 10% of the waterway width, whichever is less; in canals wholly within residential areas, the greater of 5 feet or 10%; pilings up to 15 feet beyond a dock, total no more than 65 feet or 10% - Sec. 78-159(40)g.2-3, 7
How manyNot more than one dock or mooring per lot in a residential district, except in an approved PUD or PCD - Sec. 78-159(40)g.8
PermitNo building permit for a dock until all required federal, state, regional or other agency approvals are issued - Sec. 78-159(40)i
WaiversThe dimensional limits may be waived where other agencies allow greater projections, but boathouse standards may not - Sec. 78-159(40)g
Not in the codeNo boat lift height, canopy rule or lift count was found (the only lift-related hit is davits)
Dock shapeAny 'T' or 'L' projection may be no longer than 20 feet and may not extend into the side yard setback - Sec. 78-159(40)g.6
Dock widthDocks may not be wider than ten feet - Sec. 78-159(40)g.4
BoathousesProhibited when located on an upland area - Sec. 78-159(40)g.9
State and federalFloating boat lifts and floating vessel platforms that meet s. 403.813(1)(s), F.S. (they float at all times, hold the vessel out of the water, and either sit inside a previously permitted slip or total no more than 500 sq ft - 200 sq ft in Outstanding Florida Waters - beside a qualifying dock or bulkhead) need no state permit; a local government may then ask only for a one-time registration unless the platform is attached to a bulkhead with no dock. Structures in navigable waters otherwise need Army Corps authorization unless exempt or under a general permit - 33 CFR 322.3(a).
