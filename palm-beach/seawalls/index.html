Palm Beach County · Seawalls

What are the seawall requirements in Palm Beach County?

Town by townno single county standard applies

Palm Beach is the hardest of the four counties to get a straight answer in. Broward has a county standard its cities were required to adopt; Palm Beach does not work that way. Some towns set an elevation in their own document, many set none at all, and two neighbouring towns can differ. The rule is also not always in the code where you would look for it first.

Source: City of Boca Raton Engineering Design Standards Manual § 7.7 and Code of Ordinances Ch. 22, Sec. 22-31(5); City of Delray Beach Land Development Regulations Sec. 7.1.7 and Table 7.1.7(D), Ordinance 23-21; North Palm Beach Code Sec. 5-72; Tequesta Code Sec. 76-33; Ocean Ridge Code Sec. 64-82; Gulf Stream Code Sec. 70-78 · Verified 2026-10

The detail

How this county differsBroward's cities were required to adopt one county standard. Of the Palm Beach County towns we have verified, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, North Palm Beach (Sec. 5-72), Tequesta (Sec. 76-33) and Ocean Ridge (Sec. 64-82) set a seawall elevation, each in its own code. Jupiter, West Palm Beach, Boynton Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Juno Beach, Riviera Beach, Lantana, Lake Worth Beach, South Palm Beach and Manalapan set none, and Gulf Stream sets only a maximum of 8 ft NGVD (Sec. 70-78).
Boca Raton4.0 ft NAVD88 minimum, 6.0 ft maximum. The wall must also be built so a further 12 to 18 inches can be added later without altering it.
Two documents in BocaThe 4.0 to 6.0 ft NAVD88 band is in the Engineering Design Standards Manual, section 7.7. The Code of Ordinances separately says no bulkhead may be built lower than plus 5.00 feet, with no datum stated (Sec. 22-31(5)). Ask the city in writing which it applies.
Delray Beach4.2 ft NAVD88 minimum in every flood zone, for new seawalls, substantial repairs and fixed structures attached to them (LDR 7.1.7(B)). The maximum varies with your FEMA flood zone — your base flood elevation where that is 5.0 ft or higher, 5 ft where the BFE is 4.0.
Delray's extra obligationAny contract to sell property in a tidally influenced area of Delray Beach, signed after 1 February 2022, must carry a tidal flood barrier disclosure in bold capitals of at least 14 point. The duty attaches to the sale contract (LDR 7.1.7(D)(7)).
Build low, engineer highBoth towns require a wall built under the higher figure to be designed so it can be raised later. Building to the minimum today does not close the question.
What to ask your townWhat is the minimum cap elevation, in which datum, and where is it written — the code, or an engineering standards manual? The second half of that question is the one that saves you a week.
