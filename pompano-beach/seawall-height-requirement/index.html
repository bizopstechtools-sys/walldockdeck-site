Pompano Beach · Broward County

How high does a seawall have to be in Pompano Beach?

5.0ft NAVD88 minimum

Pompano Beach sets both ends of the range. A tidal flood barrier must reach 5 ft NAVD88 and must not exceed 5 ft 10 in NAVD88, so a wall can be too high as well as too low.

Source: City of Pompano Beach Code of Ordinances § 151.05, Tidal Flood Barriers · Verified 2026-09

The detail

Minimum5 ft NAVD88
Maximum5 ft 10 in NAVD88. Both ends are enforced.
When an existing wall must be raisedOn substantial repair or rehabilitation, and when work on attached structures exceeds 50% of the barrier's cost.
How much of the wallThe whole shoreline of the property, not only the section being repaired.
AlternativesA seawall is not required where another measure is an equally effective tidal flood barrier. Automatic systems are allowed if they do not need daily human intervention.
