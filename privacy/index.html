Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to make us stop.

Last updated 2026-10-02.

Who we areWallDockDeck connects South Florida waterfront owners with licensed marine contractors. We do not perform construction ourselves.
What we collectOnly what you give us on a form: your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, and the property address. We also record which page you were on when you submitted, and which offer you responded to.
What we do not collectWe do not ask for, and have no use for, financial information, government identifiers or date of birth. If a form ever asks you for any of those, it is not ours.
Why we collect itTo send you the sheet, checklist or report you asked for, to answer your question about your property, and to arrange an inspection if you request one. The property address is what lets us give you your city’s rule rather than a generic answer.
Who we share it withA licensed marine contractor serving your area, when you ask us to arrange an inspection or a quote. We tell you who before we pass anything on. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
Our contractor partnersOnce we pass your details to a contractor, their own privacy policy governs what they do with it. Ask them for it.
EmailAsking us for a download means we will email you that download, and follow up about it. Every email has an unsubscribe link and it works immediately.
Text and phoneWe only call or text you if you tick the box giving permission, and that box is never ticked for you. See our SMS Terms for the detail.
CookiesWe keep your details in your own browser so you do not have to type them twice, and we count which pages are read so we know what to write next. We do not run advertising trackers.
How long we keep itWhile you are a live enquiry, and for a reasonable period after in case you come back. Ask us to delete it and we will.
Making us stopReply to any email, or write to us at the address below, and say what you want: stop emailing, stop calling, or delete everything. We will do it and confirm.
ChildrenThis site is for property owners and real estate professionals. It is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
ChangesIf we change this policy we change the date at the top. Material changes get an email to anyone on the list.
