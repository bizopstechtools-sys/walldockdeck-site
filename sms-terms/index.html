SMS Terms

What you are agreeing to if you tick the box to let us text you.

Last updated 2026-10-02.

Not yet in serviceWe do not currently send text messages. These terms are published so you can read them before we do, and they take effect only once we publish a number on this site. Until then, everything reaches you by email and you can stop it with the unsubscribe link in any message.
Program nameWallDockDeck Property Alerts.
What the program doesWe send text messages about the property enquiry you made: confirming an inspection time, telling you a contractor is on the way, answering a question you asked, and occasionally a notice about conditions affecting your stretch of water. We do not send marketing blasts.
How you opt inBy ticking the consent box on a form on this site, or by texting us first. The box is never ticked for you.
What you agree toThat WallDockDeck and the licensed contractor serving your area may contact you by phone, text and email about the property you told us about, including by automated means.
Consent is not a conditionEvery sheet, checklist and guide on this site is available without agreeing to be called or texted. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
Message frequencyMessage frequency varies. We text only when something is actually happening with your enquiry; there is no scheduled send.
CostsMessage and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. We do not charge you anything.

Stopping or getting help

To stopReply STOP to any message from us. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and we will not text you again unless you opt in afresh. Stopping texts does not stop email — use the unsubscribe link in any email for that.
To get helpReply HELP to any message and we will reply with how to reach us. You can also email hello@walldockdeck.com.
CarriersMobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Supported carriersMajor US carriers, including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, US Cellular, Boost, MetroPCS and Cricket. Carrier support can change.
PrivacyYour mobile information is never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
Who is contacting youThe consent text on every form names the contractor partner for your region. If it does not name anyone, do not tick it, and please tell us — that is a mistake on our side.

The exact consent wording

This is what appears beside the checkbox on our forms, so you can read it before you tick anything.

I agree that WallDockDeck and [Partner Company Name] may contact me by phone, text and email about my property at the number I have given, including by automated means.
