SMS Terms

What you are agreeing to if you tick the box to let us text you.

Last updated 2026-10-02.

What you are agreeing toIf you tick the consent box on a form, you agree that WallDockDeck and the contractor partner serving your area may contact you by phone, text and email about the property you told us about, including by automated means.
It is never ticked for youThe box starts empty. If you do not tick it, we will only email you, and only about what you asked for.
It is not a condition of anythingEvery download, sheet and checklist on this site is available without agreeing to be called or texted. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
What we would text you aboutConfirming an inspection time, telling you a contractor is on the way, or answering a question you asked. Not marketing blasts.
How oftenOnly when something is actually happening with your enquiry. There is no scheduled send.
Stopping itReply STOP to any text and it ends immediately. Reply HELP for assistance. Stopping texts does not stop email — use the unsubscribe link for that.
CostsMessage and data rates may apply, depending on your plan. We do not charge you anything.
CarriersMobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Who is callingThe consent text on the form names the contractor partner for your region. If it does not name anyone, do not tick it and tell us — that is a mistake on our side.
Contact[Your contact email] · [Your phone number]

A note on how we read this. We treat a tick on that box as permission to help you with the thing you asked about, not as permission to sell at you. If we ever get that wrong, reply STOP and we will have earned it.
