Surfside · Miami-Dade County

What are the boat lift rules in Surfside?

No lift-specific rule; marine structures 10 ft off side linesIndian Creek 35 ft, Bay 45 ft

Surfside's code does not mention boat lifts. Its rules cover 'marine structures' - a dock, pier or mooring structure - which must sit 10 feet from the waterward extension of every property line, may project only 10 feet or 10% on Point Lake and the North and South canals, and must leave a 25-foot channel with the moored boat. Before the permit issues, owners within 300 feet get mailed notice.

Source: Town of Surfside Code of Ordinances Sec. 90-57 (Supp. 55; Ord. 26-1797); Miami-Dade Code Secs. 24-48, 24-48.2 (Supp. 136); s. 403.813(1)(s), F.S. (2026); 33 CFR 322.3(a) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

ScopeSec. 90-57 governs construction of a dock, pier or mooring structure, each called a marine structure; no section names boat lifts or davits (searched lift, davit, hoist) - Code Sec. 90-57
SetbackAt least 10 feet from the waterward extension of any property line of the lot - Sec. 90-57(6)
ProjectionPoint Lake (named lots): lesser of 10% of the lot's frontage or 10 feet; other Point Lake, North Canal and South Canal lots: lesser of 10% of the waterway or 10 feet; Indian Creek: 10% up to 35 feet; Biscayne Bay: 10% up to 45 feet - Sec. 90-57(2)-(4)
ChannelNo marine structure where the dock and moored vessel together would leave less than a 25-foot-wide channel - Sec. 90-57(5)
Two frontagesOnly on the side facing the widest waterway - Sec. 90-57(1)
Neighbour noticeCourtesy notice by first-class mail, return receipt, to owners within 300 feet; no permit until 15 days after proof is filed with the town planner - Sec. 90-57(10)
Miami-Dade CountyAny work in, on or over tidal waters needs a county permit from the Department (DERM). A single-family boat lift, davit or floating dock that protrudes no more than 25% of the waterway width may use the short-form application, as may installing a boat lift or davit on a dock, seawall or bulkhead - Miami-Dade Code Secs. 24-48(1)(d), 24-48.2(I)(A)(2), (8), (20).
State and federalFloating boat lifts and floating vessel platforms that meet s. 403.813(1)(s), F.S. (they float at all times, hold the vessel out of the water, and either sit inside a previously permitted slip or total no more than 500 sq ft - 200 sq ft in Outstanding Florida Waters - beside a qualifying dock or bulkhead) need no state permit; a local government may then ask only for a one-time registration unless the platform is attached to a bulkhead with no dock. Structures in navigable waters otherwise need Army Corps authorization unless exempt or under a general permit - 33 CFR 322.3(a).
