Surfside · Miami-Dade County

What are the storm and debris rules in Surfside?

Secure or removeboats and trailers on a hurricane warning

Surfside requires boats and boat trailers stored on lots in the H30A and H30B districts to be secured or removed immediately on a hurricane warning, and portable storage units to be removed. Construction sites - including single-family and duplex jobs - must be secured on a hurricane watch or tropical storm warning, and permitted emergency repairs may be done outside normal hours with after-the-fact approval.

Source: Town of Surfside Code of Ordinances Secs. 14-32, 14-104, 90-53, 90-65 (Municode) · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Boats on lotsBoats and boat trailers stored on any lot in the H30A or H30B districts shall be secured or removed immediately upon issuance of a hurricane warning by a recognized governmental agency - Code Sec. 90-65(k); boats may be parked only on a lot with a dwelling whose occupant owns them, and no major repairs or overhauls on the premises - Sec. 90-65(i)-(j)
Construction sitesIn the event a hurricane watch or tropical storm warning is issued, the owner and contractor of a construction site, including single-family or duplex construction, shall comply with Miami-Dade County Code Sec. 8-16 by securing the site, including removing or securing hazardous or loose objects - Sec. 14-104(b)(4)
Emergency repairsEmergency repairs which require a building permit are allowed as necessary outside regular construction hours, and must be approved after the fact by the town manager or designee - Sec. 14-32(2)
Storage podsAll portable storage units shall be removed immediately upon the issuance of a hurricane warning by a recognized government agency - Sec. 90-53
