West Palm Beach · Palm Beach County

What flood zone is West Palm Beach in, and what does it require?

+2 ft above BFEor 6 in above road crown / DFE, whichever higher

West Palm Beach requires buildings and homes in flood hazard areas at base flood elevation plus 2 feet, 6 inches above the nearest road crown, or the design flood elevation, whichever is higher, and 6 inches above the road crown in Zone X. It counts improvements over any five-year period.

Source: West Palm Beach Code Ch. 18 (maintained on enCodePlus), Secs. 18-33, 18-289, 18-293; FEMA Community Status Book, Florida (www.fema.gov/cis/FL.html, report 10/03/2026); 2023 Florida Building Code, Residential R322.2.1, R322.3.2; 44 CFR 59.1; FEMA Flood Map Service Center · Verified 2026-10

The detail

FreeboardBuildings and 1-2 family dwellings/townhouses in flood hazard areas: BFE plus two feet, six inches above the nearest crown of road, or the design flood elevation, whichever is higher (buildings also ASCE 24) - Sec. 18-33(b)(1)-(2).
Zone XAt or above six inches above the nearest crown of road - Sec. 18-33(b)(3).
Manufactured homesNot less than two feet above BFE - Sec. 18-293.
Substantial improvementCumulative cost in any five year period of 50% or more of market value - Sec. 18-289.
EnclosuresEnclosed areas below the required elevation of homes and townhouses in flood hazard areas may not be partitioned or finished into rooms - Sec. 18-33(c).
Amendment historyFloodplain sections amended by Ord. No. 4729-17 (14 Sep 2017), Ord. No. 4966-21 (20 Sep 2021) and Ord. No. 5116-24 (24 Nov 2024).
More trigger points$463,000 building value -> $231,500 trigger.
FEMA flood mapFEMA's Community Status Book (report dated 3 Oct 2026) lists West Palm Beach as NFIP community 120229 with a current effective FIRM dated 20 Dec 2024. Zones are set parcel by parcel, so no single zone covers the whole city - check your own address.
NFIP historyFEMA record: first flood hazard boundary map 31 Oct 1975; first FIRM 1 Mar 1979; regular NFIP program since 1 Mar 1979; entered CRS 1 Oct 1992.
Flood insurance discountCRS class 5 - 25% off NFIP premiums for policies in the Special Flood Hazard Area (class in effect 1 Oct 2018).
State minimum2023 Florida Building Code, Residential R322.2.1 and R322.3.2 - lowest floor (A zones) or bottom of the lowest horizontal structural member (V and Coastal A zones) at BFE + 1 ft or the design flood elevation, whichever is higher.
Look up your zoneEnter the address in FEMA's Flood Map Service Center (msc.fema.gov), the official public source for FIRMs; an elevation certificate shows how your floor compares with the BFE.
