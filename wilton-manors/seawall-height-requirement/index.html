Wilton Manors · Broward County

How high does a seawall have to be in Wilton Manors?

5.0ft NAVD88

New or substantially rehabilitated seawalls, seawall caps, canal banks or berms in Wilton Manors must reach at least 5.0 ft NAVD88. Unlike the county rule, the city code has no 4.0 ft option for applications filed before 2035. The city code sets no maximum seawall height.

Source: Wilton Manors Code of Ordinances Ch. 11, Sec. 11-27 (Ord. No. 2020-002); Secs. 11-1, 11-7 and 11-29 · Verified 2026-10

The detail

Minimum5.0 ft NAVD88 for new or substantially rehabilitated seawalls, caps, canal banks or berms - Code Sec. 11-27(a)
Interimnone; no 4.0 ft option before 2035 in the city text - Sec. 11-27(a)
Maintenancerepairs due within 365 days of citation - Sec. 11-27(b)
Low wallswalls below 5.0 ft NAVD88 must not let tidal water onto neighbours or the right-of-way - Sec. 11-27(c)
Rip-raprequired at the face of new walls where none existed before - Sec. 11-27(f)
Redevelopment triggerAny application for new development, redevelopment or substantial improvement of a waterfront property must bring the shoreline up to 5.0 ft NAVD88 - Sec. 11-29
Raising the wall countsWork that changes the elevation along more than 50% of the wall's length is itself substantial repair - Sec. 11-1
Submersible liftsAny lift that can sit under water needs a guide pole at its outer end, at least 1.5 in. thick, standing 6 ft above high tide and reflective. Moored boats may take up no more than 30% of the canal width - Sec. 11-7(f)-(g)
Permit and ownershipSeawall permits come from the community development services department, with a notarized statement that you own the land abutting the water (or the owner's notarized consent). Unsafe structures found on inspection get 30 days to fix - Sec. 11-7(b), (e)
